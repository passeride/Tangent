About these views

MIDI reading views use a sharp-based pitch spelling and approximate duration symbols. They are not an engraved score. Staff lanes rotate the two five-line staves into the falling direction, preserving alignment with the piano. Falling noteheads wait at the strike line for their key-down duration. The sheet view uses a moving playhead and advances by page. Audio is a lightweight synthesized piano; percussion channels are silent. MusicXML engraving and sampled pianos are future work.
